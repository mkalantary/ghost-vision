# Using Ghost-Vision responsibly

Ghost-Vision was built to be **privacy-respecting** — it detects presence without cameras and records no images, sound or identities. It can still detect people, and people have a right to know when they're being sensed. Please use it with the same care you'd use with any security or monitoring equipment.

## Please do

- **Use it in spaces you own or are responsible for**, such as your home, your lab, or a workplace where you have permission.
- **Tell people.** Anyone who lives in, works in or regularly visits the space should know a presence sensor is installed. A simple sign is often enough.
- **Check your local laws.** Rules about monitoring people — especially employees, tenants and visitors — differ between countries and regions. When in doubt, ask.
- **Keep the sensing network private.** Use a strong WiFi password, and don't connect the sensing router to the internet.
- **Look after the recordings.** They contain no images, but they do show when a space was occupied. Store them carefully and delete them when you no longer need them.

## Please don't

- Use it to sense people in spaces you don't control, or without their knowledge.
- Use it to monitor someone in a way that would make them feel unsafe or controlled.
- Rely on it alone where safety is at stake. Detection rates haven't been field-measured yet, and like every sensor, it can miss things.
- Describe it as more than it is. It detects motion and presence; it doesn't identify, image or count people. Overclaiming harms the people who trust you.

## Good uses this project was designed for

- Knowing whether a room is occupied — for lighting, heating or energy saving — without putting a camera in it
- Detecting someone entering a space that should be empty, such as a server room or storeroom after hours
- Learning about WiFi sensing, radio physics and embedded systems
- Research that needs open, documented, reproducible raw data
