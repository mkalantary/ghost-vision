# Code of conduct

Ghost-Vision welcomes everyone — beginners and experts, from every country and background. We want this to be a place where asking a "simple" question feels completely normal.

## What we expect

- **Be kind and patient**, especially with people who are new. Everyone was a beginner once.
- **Assume good intentions.** Most misunderstandings are just that.
- **Criticise ideas, not people.** "This change made detection worse in my room, here are my numbers" is helpful. Personal remarks are not.
- **Back up claims with evidence**, and accept evidence that shows you were wrong. That's how this project has improved.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't post other people's personal information, and don't share real network passwords or addresses, even your own.

## What isn't acceptable

- Harassment, insults or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or behaviour of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
- Asking for help using this project to sense people without their knowledge, or otherwise against [responsible use](docs/responsible-use.md)

## If something goes wrong

If someone's behaviour makes you uncomfortable, contact the maintainer privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or block people who don't follow this code.
